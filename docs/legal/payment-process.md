# Payment Process / 支付流程

**Last updated / 最后更新：14 August 2026 / 2026年8月14日**

## Customer payment steps / 客户支付步骤

1. **Register and sign in / 注册并登录**  
   Create an account, complete any required verification, and sign in to the customer console.  
   创建账户、完成所需验证并登录客户控制台。

2. **Open the purchase page / 进入充值页面**  
   Select the account top-up or available purchase option.  
   选择账户充值或当前可用的购买选项。

3. **Choose the amount / 选择金额**  
   Review the amount, payment currency, account receiving the balance, and any limits displayed on the page.  
   核对页面展示的金额、支付币种、余额接收账户及适用限额。

4. **Choose a payment method / 选择支付方式**  
   The checkout page displays only methods currently enabled for the customer and transaction. Availability may vary by country, device, currency, amount, and payment-provider rules.  
   结算页面仅展示当前客户及交易可用的支付方式；可用方式可能因国家、设备、币种、金额及支付服务商规则而异。

5. **Review and confirm / 核对并确认**  
   Before payment, review the final amount, currency, order information, and links to the Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Digital Delivery Policy, and Refund Policy.  
   支付前，请核对最终金额、币种、订单信息，并阅读用户服务协议、隐私政策、数字交付政策及退款政策。

6. **Complete provider authentication / 完成支付验证**  
   Follow the payment provider's page, QR code, bank, wallet, or card authentication instructions. Do not close the page until a result is shown.  
   按支付服务商页面、二维码、银行、钱包或银行卡验证指引完成操作，在显示结果前请勿关闭页面。

7. **Receive digital delivery / 接收数字交付**  
   After successful confirmation, the order status is updated and the purchased balance is credited to the account. You can review the result in the payment-result page or order history.  
   支付成功确认后，订单状态将更新，所购余额记入账户。您可在支付结果页或订单记录中查看结果。

8. **Use the service and review records / 使用服务并查看记录**  
   Create or manage API keys, call enabled models, and review usage and billing records in the console.  
   在控制台创建或管理 API Key、调用已启用模型，并查看用量及计费记录。

## Payment troubleshooting / 支付问题处理

- **Pending:** wait for the provider confirmation, then refresh the payment-result or order-history page.  
  **处理中：**等待支付服务商确认后，刷新支付结果页或订单记录。
- **Failed or cancelled:** no balance should be delivered; start a new order only after confirming the earlier order was not charged.  
  **失败或取消：**通常不会交付余额；确认原订单未扣款后再发起新订单。
- **Charged but not credited:** contact [service@tokenapifuel.com](mailto:service@tokenapifuel.com) from the registered email and provide the order number, time, amount, currency, and redacted receipt.  
  **已扣款但未入账：**使用注册邮箱联系客服并提供订单号、时间、金额、币种及已遮盖敏感信息的凭证。
- **Refund:** follow the [Refund Policy](/legal/refund-policy).  
  **退款：**请按[退换货及退款政策](/legal/refund-policy)提交申请。

## Security reminders / 安全提示

We will never ask you by email or chat for your password, full card number, card security code, or secret API key. Confirm that you are on the official website before entering payment information.

我们不会通过邮件或聊天索要您的密码、完整银行卡号、银行卡安全码或秘密 API Key。输入支付信息前，请确认您正在访问官方网站。
