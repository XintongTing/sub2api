# Refund Policy / 退换货及退款政策

**Effective date / 生效日期：14 August 2026 / 2026年8月14日**

Because the website provides digital services and does not sell physical goods, physical returns and exchanges do not apply. This policy explains when a payment or unused service balance may be refunded. Mandatory rights under applicable consumer law remain unaffected.

本网站提供数字化服务且不销售实体商品，因此不适用实体退货或换货。本政策说明付款或未使用服务余额可申请退款的情形。适用消费者法律规定的强制性权利不受影响。

## 1. Refund-eligible cases / 可申请退款的情形

You may request a review when:

- the same order was charged more than once because of a verified payment or system error;
- payment was completed but the purchased balance was not delivered and we cannot complete delivery within a reasonable period;
- an unauthorised payment is promptly reported and confirmed after security review;
- the platform's entire API service is continuously unavailable for more than 72 hours due to a fault within our reasonable control, in which case the unused balance may be refunded or the service period extended; or
- a refund is otherwise required by applicable law.

出现以下情形时，您可申请审核：同一订单因经核实的支付或系统错误被重复扣款；付款完成但所购余额未交付且我们无法在合理期限内完成交付；未经授权的付款被及时报告并经安全审核确认；因我们合理控制范围内的故障导致平台全部 API 服务连续超过 72 小时不可用，此时可申请退还未使用余额或延长服务期；或适用法律另有退款要求。

## 2. Normally non-refundable cases / 通常不予退款的情形

Except where required by law, refunds are normally unavailable for:

- service balance already consumed through API usage;
- dissatisfaction with the content, style, accuracy, or suitability of third-party AI model output;
- failure caused by the customer's code, device, network, leaked credentials, incorrect model parameters, or breach of the Terms of Service;
- a mistaken top-up made to the account that initiated and received the order, after digital balance has been delivered;
- upstream price, rate-limit, model, or availability changes outside our reasonable control; or
- suspension or termination resulting from fraud, abuse, unlawful use, or a material policy breach.

除法律另有要求外，下列情形通常不予退款：已通过 API 调用消耗的服务余额；对第三方 AI 模型输出的内容、风格、准确性或适用性不满意；因客户代码、设备、网络、凭据泄露、模型参数错误或违反用户服务协议导致的失败；数字余额已交付后，误充值至发起并接收该订单的账户；超出我们合理控制范围的上游价格、限流、模型或可用性变化；以及因欺诈、滥用、违法使用或重大违规导致的暂停或终止。

## 3. How to request a refund / 退款申请方式

Send an email from the account's registered address to [service@tokenapifuel.com](mailto:service@tokenapifuel.com) with the subject "Refund Request / 退款申请". Include:

1. registered account email;
2. order number and payment date;
3. amount and currency;
4. reason for the request; and
5. supporting evidence, with sensitive payment data redacted.

请使用账户注册邮箱发送邮件至 [service@tokenapifuel.com](mailto:service@tokenapifuel.com)，主题注明“Refund Request / 退款申请”，并提供：注册邮箱、订单号及支付日期、金额及币种、申请原因，以及已遮盖支付敏感信息的证明材料。

Do not send passwords, full payment-card numbers, card security codes, or API keys.

请勿发送密码、完整银行卡号、银行卡安全码或 API Key。

## 4. Review and payment / 审核及退款到账

We normally acknowledge a complete request within three business days. We aim to complete eligibility review within 15 business days. Approved refunds are normally returned to the original payment method within seven business days after approval, but the bank or payment provider may require additional processing time. If the original method cannot accept a refund, we will contact you regarding a lawful alternative.

资料完整的申请通常在三个工作日内得到确认，我们力争在十五个工作日内完成资格审核。获批退款通常在批准后七个工作日内原路退回，但银行或支付服务商可能需要额外处理时间。如原支付方式无法接收退款，我们会联系您确认合法的替代方案。

Processing fees already retained by a payment provider may be deducted where permitted by law and disclosed before the refund is completed. We do not charge a separate fee when the refund results from our duplicate charge or delivery failure.

在法律允许且退款完成前已向您说明的情况下，支付服务商已收取且不退还的处理费可能从退款中扣除。因我们重复扣款或交付失败导致的退款，我们不另收退款手续费。
